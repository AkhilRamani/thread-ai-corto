# **Take-Home Assignment**

**MySubscriptions: A Connected Services Dashboard with AI Insights** 

**Duration:** Estimated 4–8 hours    | **Submission Format:** git repository link + hosted link (preferred) 

## **Objective**

Build a small web application called **MySubscriptions** . 

The app lets a user connect one or more of their online services, retrieves their recent data, and uses AI to tell the user something interesting about it. This challenge is designed to evaluate your ability to: 

- Integrate with cloud-based services and APIs 
- Use Authentication securely and effectively 
- Apply AI processing where it genuinely adds value 
- Build clear, usable interfaces



## **Requirements**

### **1. Authentication & Service Connection**

Let a user sign in to the app and **connect** at least **one** compatible service. It can be the same provider or something else 

Pick a service whose data you’d actually enjoy working with. A few examples (but not limited to): 

- Spotify: playlists, tracks, listening history 
- Reddit: posts, comments, saved items 
- Google Calendar: events 
- Strava or Fitbit: activities and workouts 
- YouTube: subscriptions, playlists 
- Notion, Dropbox, Google Drive: documents and files 
- GitHub: repositories, issues, commits

### **2. Retrieve & Present the Data**

Once a service is connected, pull in a meaningful slice of the user’s recent data and present it. What’s worth showing, and how you surface it, is your call — there’s no required layout, item count, or set of fields. 

### **3. AI Insights**

This is the heart of the exercise. Use an LLM to do something genuinely interesting with the data you’ve pulled. We’re far more curious about how you explore the data, explore the model’s capabilities, and show us your thinking. 

### **4. Future Enhancements**

If you were given 1/5/20 more days, what enhancements would you plan? Please describe — maybe in the README.md or separately. 

## **Optional Enhancements**

- Support multiple services (e.g., Spotify + Google Calendar) 
- Visualisations of your AI output 
- Backend API separation



## **Submission Instructions**
